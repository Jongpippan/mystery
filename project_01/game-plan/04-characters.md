# People, first encounters and relationships

Version: `plan-v1`. Character design is author guidance, not already authored dialogue. Public names below are exact Korean spellings. No inherited cast or series IDs. Actual utterance IDs are allocated by screenplay batch; voice examples here are register fragments, not accepted script.

## P00 — 나여백 (34)

Records conservator hired to inventory paper holdings. Default name 나여백; player display-name substitution uses stable P00 and `{playerName}` only, without changing profession, age or family history. Maternal aunt/uncle gender is fixed here as **aunt**: P01 calls her **이모**, not the entered name. Ordinary adults use `{playerName} 씨`; role-based address is allowed. P00 is not a licensed fictional detective or police official. P07 authorizes assisted record comparison and safe demonstrations while retaining evidence custody.

She places a tiny 'pending' slip under objects people are still discussing, including a cup someone is actively drinking. Once she starts a list, she wants an actual ending ceremony for it and will queue for a queue she invented. This inconveniences work rather than merely producing sharp remarks. Strength: provenance and listening for precise scope. Blind spot: believes a form protects people even when nobody understood it. Wants a clean paid inventory and a tolerable weekend with P01; later must admit her own early record of P02's claim overstated certainty. Register: full but not formal sentences, cautious corrections such as **“제가 본 건 거기까지예요.”** No automatic solution monologue.

Relationships: P01's mother is P00's older sister, offscreen and informed by phone; P00 has agreed responsibility for the weekend. P02 commissioned the work and resents forms being attached to hotel furniture. P08 respects her provenance work but treats her as a receiving clerk until challenged. P07 respects careful boundaries rather than granting implausible authority.

First encounter is the player arriving, unpacking an excessive set of provisional labels while P01 needs a toilet. Change: from preserving papers to preserving consent and uncertainty. Branch ending: attributed individual exhibit versus jointly annotated exhibit, both honest.

## P01 — 나모눈 (11)

P00's niece; sixth-grade primary-school child with permission to accompany her aunt during a weekend job extending through Monday under a prearranged school experiential-learning absence. Her mother knows the lodging and itinerary; P00/P01 share the ordinary town guesthouse arranged for the job. School interest: record sounds of a place about to close, only with consent. Exact age never slides upward to justify adult behavior.

She runs an imaginary lost-property counter for things that cannot arrive at one: an echo, a squeaky chair, an unclaimed smell. She insists on ticket order, then lets a broken object cut the line because it 'cannot wait standing'. This causes practical rearrangement and irritates P00/P02. She can be stubbornly unfair in a small childlike way, lose interest, miss an adult implication, and apologize without turning into a moral lecturer. Register toward P00: **“이모, 얘 차례 아직 안 끝났어.”** Toward unfamiliar adults polite but too literal. No catchphrase quota; no joke in every line.

Competence: remembers timbre and placement of her own sound samples, notices that two labels name the same chair squeak. Limits: cannot authenticate recording metadata, read financing documents unaided or infer murder from posture. She shares a noticed repetition, not its logical answer. Wants permission to put voices in her project; learns that being heard is different from being recorded.

Relationships: P00 supervises and sometimes overdocuments her; P02 takes the imaginary counter seriously enough to file an absurd complaint; P05 gives her a safe microphone demonstration and later owes her an honest explanation about reuse. Her independent scenes concern consent and boundaries, not covert participation. During police/landing/crate lifting activity P02 supervises her in L02; P07 and P00 do hazardous work. All essential information remains accessible if she is absent from a scene.

## P02 — 봉만실 (62)

Owner of 여울관. Preparing a last gathering while trying to keep the sale and staff loan intelligible to no one but herself. She retires worn umbrellas by giving them hotel room numbers, then refuses to let dry guests borrow one because that umbrella is 'off duty'. The resulting wet queue inconveniences staff and later makes raincoat availability worth asking about. No need to explain the habit with trauma.

Wants staff paid and hotel remembered as generous; fears that admitting a loan makes every past kindness seem purchased. Authorized one loan, not diversion. Knows wall move and her own glimpses. Initially upgrades a sleeve into an identified person. Cooperates on physical facts, resists unpicking staff consent.

Relations: hired P03 and repeatedly delegated uncomfortable explanations to him; has treated P06 as a child despite relying on her wages work; shares long repairs history with P04; commissioned P00. First appears alone at reception actually arranging wet umbrellas while P00 needs check-in instructions. Reappears because she must prepare food, supervise P01 or answer staff questions; no teleporting assembly.

Register: hospitable imperatives and sudden fussy corrections, **“젖은 건 여기 놓아요. 우산 말고 사람부터요.”** Change: publicly separates what she authorized from what people understood, accepts her own responsibility without confessing someone else's act.

## P03 — 차무록 (43)

Operations/accounts manager, culprit. Counts used staples into a 'recoverable' jar and asks people to initial a subtraction if a staple bends. He interrupts an emotionally charged scene to recover a perfectly reusable clip. This is a foolish priority with visible social costs, not a charming mastermind tic; his ordinary interactions must still be varied.

Wants his control of accounts recognized as the hotel's real work; fears being revealed as someone who used other people's insecurity as collateral. Controlled the contractor account, substituted pages, pushed P08, then altered the disclosure copy and backdated coat custody. Ability: routine forms, work schedules and access, not superhuman planning. Failure: assumed wet bound originals were useless and that nobody would distinguish a garment from its current wearer.

Relations: P02's dependence enabled him; P06 is threatened through her unauthorized pledge; P08 threatens exposure; P04 has repeatedly taken small favors without asking their price. First appears later than P02, carrying the invoice P00 actually needs and trying to salvage clips from P00's unopened packet. He does real work before suspicion. Register: **“그 칸은 비워 두시면 안 됩니다.”** Avoid ominous smiles or metadata that identifies him as guilty. Ending emotional account is a claim, not retroactive evidence.

## P04 — 탁두철 (38)

Maintenance contractor. P06's older cousin; P05's former spouse, now an awkward practical friend. Wants to finish safe closure work and protect P06's records. He rehearses instructions to a trolley before allowing a person to use it, then scolds the person for not hearing the trolley's answer. He names tools only when annoyed and cannot keep the names straight. Competent at real mechanics, foolish about delegating social problems to objects.

Secrets/faults: failed to close a maintenance gate; helped remove staff records and burn misleading duplicates; frightened that admitting one fault will be treated as admitting the murder. Did not know P03's plan or the coat's history. Gives coat handoff information when asked; do not make him withhold it arbitrarily. Mistaken early clock labels come from the edited copy, not his certainty about a watch.

First encounter: fixing a stuck partition wheel in L03 after the night, asks P00 to hold a harmless work tag while he speaks to the wheel. P07 controls reenactment. His physical explanation is free and truthful; player proves its significance. Relations with P03 concern small favors and obligations; with P02, years of postponed repair requests. Register: **“움직이는 건 맞는데, 저절로는 안 움직여요.”** Later apologizes to a person without first addressing equipment.

## P05 — 소해금 (31)

Event sound technician hired for the last gathering. Wants an orderly final performance and payment. She maintains a strict moment of silence for failed microphones, then accidentally leaves a good one live while telling everyone to respect the silence. Her confidence concerns sound work; she cannot diagnose identity from a blurred silhouette.

Recorded P08 at rehearsal and replayed without final permission. Saw an advance and two-arm push-shaped action through the frosted pane; no face or reliable coat color visible there. Lies about live speech, then understates her visual observation because she fears a chain from her lie to a false accusation against P04. Q05 correction must be specific and limited, not a sudden identification.

Relations: former spouse P04, residual help mixed with irritation; mentor for P01's permitted sound project; professional clash with P08 over who gets to authorize his voice. First encounter before the incident: demonstrating why a booth microphone is muted, then accidentally amplifying her instructions. No crime disclosure. Register: technical words followed by concrete demonstration; **“여기 켜져 있어도 저쪽에는 안 나가요. 지금은요.”** Learns to distinguish uncertainty from silence and accepts harm from the unauthorized playback.

## P06 — 배한술 (27)

Kitchen worker and staff representative, P04's cousin. Cooks while assembling wage claims; labels tiny leftover dishes as 'full committee' and keeps reconvening them when someone eats a member. Refuses to sit in the chair reserved for 'staff', although she is the representative, because nobody has voted the chair vacant. These decisions materially delay meals and meetings, rather than decorating a suspect biography.

Wants staff paid now; fears that an unauthorized second pledge will make colleagues distrust all her work. Removed real ledgers and burned cover duplicates with P04. No murder knowledge. Her denial of cargo is an actual lie; after Q03 she tells the route and participants honestly. Both her and P04's reported 20:07–20:17 handoff times initially derive from the same copy, not independent clocks. Preserve this distinction in dialogue.

Relations: P02's paternalism, P03's leverage, P04's overprotective help, P09's custody bureaucracy. First foreground encounter CH03 while rescuing a dinner service from her own committee labels; earlier background kitchen activity has no named-person registration. Register: **“대표라고 제 몫까지 동의한 건 아니에요.”** Change: asks staff to decide on disclosed terms instead of privately solving their finances.

## P07 — 진새벽 (46)

Responding police investigator, arrives after emergency call, controls crime-site access and evidence custody. No prearrival omniscience. Will listen to P00's records comparisons and organize safe demonstrations because P00 is both a witness and relevant conservator. Formal accusation stays an evidence briefing; adults can refuse unsafe requests without withholding ordinary facts.

Habit: writes a question on a fresh card before asking it, then loses the card under the answer. Lets a person finish an irrelevant correction because she cannot bear an incorrectly labeled envelope. Not every principal must be equally absurd; her orderly seriousness lets other behavior have consequences.

Relations: P00 must distinguish observation from inference; P02 is a witness and safe-area supervisor, not an assistant detective; P04 is responsible for access safety even when suspected. First encounter after P01 is escorted to L02: establishes who saw what and what work is permitted, without an exposition wall of legal detail. Register: **“보신 것과 그렇게 생각하신 이유를 따로 적겠습니다.”** Ending retains uncertainty about judicial outcomes, no magical authority payoff.

## P08 — 표문식 (51)

Independent accountant engaged to settle closure accounts, victim. Wants originals transferred before morning and believes that obtaining every signature is the same as explaining a decision. Refuses an ordinary chair because he has designated it a temporary 'inbox' and then stands through a long meeting complaining about his feet. His absence changes the room's behavior later; do not reduce him to corpse evidence.

Relations: respects P00's preservation work but gives her little room to speak; conflicts with P03 over missing funds; P06 resents being treated as a signature source; P05 objects to his rehearsal edits. First encounter in a separate quiet inventory scene before death, with a concrete task and minor disagreement, not a parade of suspects. Register: careful lengthy qualification followed by impatient practical decisions. Final unsent note may add regret but cannot disclose a decisive new crime fact after accusation.

## P09 — 목백로 (56)

Hotel's regular cargo-boat operator and temporary record custodian. Uses a sand timer for breaks and writes a receipt when lending it, then interrupts the break to demand the receipt back. Takes seals seriously but delegated one embarrassing redaction task to P03. Not a secret late witness: introduced in the prologue during an actual delivery and custody-format demonstration.

Supervised P04/P06 continuously during the handoff; escorts and seals cargo; does not know the true fall time. His claimed clock interval later comes from the disclosure copy. Lies only about supervising P03's copying to avoid admitting his custody breach. Q06 unlocks copier access, not retroactively invented knowledge of a murder. Relations: respects P06's staff purpose but dislikes her shortcuts; long working history with P04; uneasy indebtedness to P02; trusts P03's formal masks too much.

Register: **“받았다는 거하고, 안에 뭘 봤다는 건 달라요.”** Change: admits exactly what he failed to observe. His false assurance is consequential, not a punchline when the case turns on it.

## Introduction and relation coverage

First foreground order: P00/P01 family arrival → P02 reception → practical orientation and quiet interval → P03 work packet → separate P08 inventory → P05 booth demonstration → P09 delivery → public activity/death response → P07 → next morning P04 → CH03 P06. Prologue is multiple batches, not one short scene introducing everyone. A background worker is not automatically a registered named person.

Every chapter has both protagonist/child exchanges and NPC-to-NPC action: CH01 P02/P04 disagree over the requested wall setting; CH02 P05/P03 argue over who authorized playback; CH03 P06/P04/P09 negotiate real cargo custody; CH04 P02/P06 confront delegated consent; CH05 P05/P04 face gate responsibility without assuming guilt; CH06 P09/P03 confront record custody, followed by P02/P06 discussing compensation accountability. Optional scenes change relationships, never own the only essential fact.
