Authentic 20:14–20:24 with one-minute tolerance guarantees 20:15–20:23, covering the incident window. P04/P06 are excluded as actors by continuous presence, not walking speed or cargo innocence. P03's early garment custody, access/meeting, supported motive, manipulated derivative and defeated strongest P04 opportunity combine for attribution; neither weak alibi nor confession alone decides the case. Other people's loan, pledge, removal, burning and gate responsibilities survive.

Physical review retains fixed doors/window and x6/x9 partition, public exit route without wall crossing, cart-turn limitation only for the intact cart, post-incident/pre-discovery sealing, one bound lower original, separate ordinary copier and clock-impression device, and supervised child activity in all relevant branches.
