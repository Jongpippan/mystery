# Concept and dramatic decisions

Version: `plan-v1`. All facts planned; truth authority is `02-truth-SPOILERS.md`.

## Setting

여울관 is a small coastal hotel in a fictional contemporary Korean town. It has stopped taking overnight bookings and is holding one final staff-and-former-guest gathering before a sale. The empty indoor pool is a closed maintenance area; the functioning ballroom and kitchen share an older service network. Stormy weather explains raincoats and interrupted outdoor work, not total isolation or an absence of functioning police/phones. There are no secret twins, supernatural rules, biometric identification or magical forensic measurements.

P00 has been hired for a three-day inventory of hotel and staff records. P02 arranged an ordinary guesthouse in town for P00/P01, outside the investigated hotel site; no overnight hotel booking is implied. They stay together, and overnight departure/return is a clearly narrated transition, not a secret investigation route. The work is a supervised recordkeeping job, not a treasure hunt. P01 accompanies P00 over the weekend with her mother's consent and a prearranged school experiential-learning absence for Monday; she expects to catalog abandoned hotel sounds for a school project. P00 initially mistakes precise documentation for neutrality. P01 initially treats forgotten objects as customers whose complaints must be heard. Their growth is learning when an observation needs another person's consent and when protecting someone by silently deciding for them becomes harm.

## Surface and escalation

The prologue establishes arrival, contract, family supervision and the hotel's immediate activity. The first question arises only after a death report and a visibly incompatible account of the same room. Prologue ends before the main case has been explained or solved.

The six mysteries belong to different causal systems: space; recorded speech/time; transport and salvage; documentary authority/motive; eyewitness perception and physical action; custody and attribution. The partition investigation resolves entirely in CH01. Later chapters reuse its established routes without pretending to rediscover the trick.

There is one fatal act. Other wrongdoing is real: unauthorized use of a recorded voice, removal of archives, deceptive destruction of duplicates, and a second unauthorized pledge of a compensation fund. Those actions obstruct observation, alter routes, produce motives and damage trust. Clearing a person of the fatal act does not erase their choices.

## Emotional resolution

The player proves that P03 pushed P08 and then redirected suspicion through records and clothing. The ending distinguishes evidence-supported acts from the suspect's uncorroborated explanation of his feelings. P08 intended to expose misappropriation but had also treated staff as signatures to be collected; he is neither a saint nor a punchline.

Common outcome: the death and major concealments are explained; evidence is handed to P07; the sale/compensation documents undergo independent review within the fiction. No guaranteed legal outcome or magically restored hotel is promised. Relationship choices determine whether P00 prepares the staff's exhibit as attributed personal accounts or a jointly annotated document, and whether P01's sound project includes voices with consent or only environmental recordings. Neither branch changes the culprit, child innocence or proof access.

## Design choices and rejected shortcuts

- Adopt the hotel and a movable partition, but invent their actual dimensions, operations and people here. Do not treat the earlier fixture as a hidden reference.
- Use an ordinary physical fall and eyewitness limits rather than a specialized poison or calculated time of death. The player proves an incident window from independent event records; no exact medical death minute is required.
- Start the late custody problem early: wet documents are identified and conserved in CH03, record formats demonstrated before discrepancies matter, and a raincoat's condition is recorded at recovery. CH06 releases already caused work, not a new omniscient laboratory report.
- Keep the initial colleague-suspect plausible after the time correction. His final exclusion requires a recovered original, authentication, a witness correction and a measured minimum route; an accusation against P03 alone is insufficient.
- No early packet may invite the writer to invent missing essential proof. Source conflicts block the affected assignment and return to Codex for repair.
