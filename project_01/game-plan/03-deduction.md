## Judgment conventions

- Inputs include a proposition, relevant E/S references and the connecting reason. A valid set must cover every listed role, not equal one ordered ID list. Supersets of relevant evidence are allowed; unrelated extras neither help nor invalidate otherwise adequate reasoning unless the committed reason makes a false claim.
- Source substitutions are explicitly listed below. A record and its transcript are two views of one source. E30/E49 are stages of one object. Do not double-count them as independent corroboration.
- Evidence-link tasks accept any ordering unless temporal/causal order is the question. Timeline tasks accept interval constraints and equivalent topological order, not guessed minute values.
- Every D has K of the same number (`D01→K01`); every Q has `KQ01` etc plus its new E. The Korean K summary is drafted as the successful player's claim, not companion auto-explanation.
- Input incomplete: free block. Complete wrong logical submission: one visible policy cost. Feedback distinguishes the specific unsupported premise from a relationship choice. All tools and editable drafts remain available.
- Each listed prerequisite has a concrete action in the evidence ledger or a prior task. Mandatory acquisition cannot depend on selecting the 'nice' relationship option.
