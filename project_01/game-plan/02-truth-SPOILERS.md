## Fixed responsibility

**T01. Fatal act.** At approximately 20:19 on October 21, P03 차무록 pushes P08 표문식 with both hands on the upper pool service landing, L09. P08 falls through the already open maintenance gate into the empty pool. P03 had arranged a private meeting there, intending to stop P08 handing over the real compensation accounts. The encounter was not an authorized reenactment. P03 then leaves rather than summoning help. The reconstruction proves the act and deliberate concealment; the game does not demand an exact physiological time of death.

**T02. Conditions and limits.** P04 had legitimately opened the gate that afternoon for adult maintenance work and attached a visible warning. He failed to close it after stopping work, creating an unsafe condition for which he must answer. The floor is dry at the landing; rain is confined to the exterior service route. No timer, hidden trap, altered friction or impossible falling trajectory is involved. An open gate and a quarrel permit an accident explanation until the witness account is challenged and checked. The witness is evidence of the push; gate condition alone never proves it.

**T03. Main motive.** P03 diverted staff compensation money to a contractor account he controlled and produced false withdrawal/consent sheets. P08 discovered incompatible originals and demanded that the originals be inventoried the next morning. P03 feared loss of position, repayment and exposure. P08's surviving meeting card, an already sent itemized discrepancy letter, authenticated account schedules and P03's access establish this motive before final accusation. P03's ending speech may explain his self-justification; it supplies no missing necessary fact.

**T04. Coat and concealment.** P03 wears the hotel's one red raincoat with a triangular repair on its left lapel at 20:16. During the push, P08 grips that lapel and tears off a strip; the strip remains with the body and is recovered by P07. At about 20:26, P03 offers the now-torn coat to soaked P04 at the service rack. P04 accepts without knowledge of the death. Later P04 admits this handoff when the coat is raised; he does not irrationally conceal an ordinary fact until CH06. The strip links a coat to the incident, not its later wearer to murder. P03 claims he lent it before 20:17; this is a deliberately false statement and the strongest late alternative is that P04 borrowed it and pushed P08.

**T05. False late attribution.** P03 changes both start/end times on a disclosure copy of a record handoff from 20:14–20:24 to 20:07–20:17. This makes P04 appear free during the fall. P09 had supervised the real ten-minute handoff without either P04 or P06 leaving; he remembers the interval and participants directly but later takes the printed copy's clock times as accurate. The receiving form has a loose upper counterpart and a bound pressure-copy lower counterpart, both impressed during the same start/end actions. P09 retains the loose counterpart; the bound one travels inside the cargo ledger. P09 later allows P03 to make the disclosure copy from the loose counterpart alone to hide wage amounts, contrary to his own custody rule. P03 substitutes the two times in the public copy and destroys the loose counterpart rather than returning it. The player must prove the altered copy and failed return/custody; the exact destruction action is author truth, not an extra graded conclusion requiring nonexistent forensic evidence. Q06 forces him to admit that access. The original duplicate was already bound into the cargo's ledger and sealed before the body was discovered; it survives the wet-cargo incident. Its recovery, authentication and comparison are CH06's substantial local mystery. Do not introduce a new late witness, recording capability or laboratory oracle.
