## Experience and boundaries

A records conservator arrives with an eleven-year-old niece to inventory a closing hotel. An accountant dies during the final evening. The player reconstructs not only who caused his fall but why other people manufactured mutually incompatible histories that night. A displaced partition, a reused voice announcement, an apparently human cargo, forged staff consent, a misdescribed fall, and an altered evidence handoff each receive a full chapter's investigation and local resolution.

Murder is treated seriously and without graphic injury imagery. The child remains in safe public investigation areas or with a named responsible adult; dangerous inspection and official evidence handling are performed by adults. Police authority and the conservator's agreed observational role are introduced explicitly. The game's conclusion is an evidential reconstruction handed to the investigator, not an assertion of a court verdict.

Tone: socially inconvenient eccentricity, tense cooperation and grief; jokes concern habits and conflicting priorities, never the victim's injury. Theme: a record can preserve a person's voice or erase their agency. The hotel sale need not be undone for the case to resolve.

## Production steps, risks and rollback

1. Design all chapters and people with physical/evidence ledgers; review full backward proof and alternate orders.
2. Export one consolidated full-script packet with full author truth and bounded internal writing units. Review the assembled return and consolidate necessary revisions. Maintain full-game coverage throughout.
3. Review assembled chapters and the whole screenplay; adapt only after G0/G1 readiness.
4. Select actual runtime, create a representative integration slice, extend the full game, then execute G2/G3/G5 and available independent G4.

Main risks: a weak late alibi manufactured by a convenient new document; witness interpretation masquerading as physical proof; all eccentric people sounding equally witty; local scene compression reducing scope; restoring unseen future facts through UI. Mitigations are the early planting, source independence and staged knowledge reviews in the ledgers. No critical inference may depend on assumed forensic expertise.

Rollback: preserve packet revisions and candidate returns. Revise authoritative planning first, identify dependent D/Q/C/E, then regenerate a new packet version. Never silently change a source already sent to the writer. No runtime rollback is applicable yet. Do not delete user files or commit automatically.

## Scale and platform

Content scale comes from the designed scene/challenge coverage, not inherited quotas. Reading time remains unestimated until a full draft supplies actual Korean text; inference and navigation time require separate play observations. A guessed playtime is not an acceptance budget.

Target interaction: browser, desktop keyboard/mouse and phone touch, local save/resume and readable enlarged Korean. Stack selection and actual commands are deferred until script readiness; no paid tool, account, backend or deployment is assumed.
